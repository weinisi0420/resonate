Resonate
A neighborhood app that helps apartment residents connect, support one another, and make the most of shared spaces.
Resonate brings everyday community life into one place. Neighbors can share updates, find people with common interests, ask for or offer help, exchange items, send messages, and check or book shared facilities.

Features
- Vibe Match — discover neighbors with shared interests.
- Neighborhood feed — share moments and updates with your community.
- Bridge of Needs — ask for a favor or offer a hand.
- Community marketplace — share items and coordinate exchanges.
- Messaging and profiles — connect and chat with neighbors.
- Facilities and neighborhood map — explore shared spaces and manage bookings.
- Voice Box — submit feedback about the property.
